# Multi-Scope AI Budgets & Hard Limits

Manages daily, weekly, monthly, and custom budget limits (`AIBudget`) across organization, workspace, team, agent, and mission scopes.

## Limit Enforcement
- **Soft Limits**: Triggers early warning notifications at 50%, 75%, and 90% consumption.
- **Hard Limits**: Executes policy-controlled actions (`block`, `pause`, `require_approval`, `degrade`).
