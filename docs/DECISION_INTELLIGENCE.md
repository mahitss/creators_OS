# Enterprise Decision Intelligence Architecture

## Decision Intelligence Pipeline
RAW EVENTS $\rightarrow$ NORMALIZED SIGNALS $\rightarrow$ FEATURES $\rightarrow$ TRENDS $\rightarrow$ ANOMALY DETECTION $\rightarrow$ FORECAST $\rightarrow$ SCENARIOS $\rightarrow$ RECOMMENDATION $\rightarrow$ DECISION $\rightarrow$ EXECUTION $\rightarrow$ OUTCOME $\rightarrow$ LEARNING.

## Critical Safeguards & Language Rules
- **Predictions are NOT facts**: Forecasts explicitly include source, time window, method, uncertainty ranges, freshness, and assumptions. Speculation is never presented as certainty.
- **Recommendations are NOT decisions**: AI suggestions are NOT authorization.
- **Forbidden Language**: Never claim "Vapor knows what will happen" or "AI guarantees this outcome". Use "Forecast", "Projected", "Estimated", "Based on historical signals", "Scenario", and "Recommendation".
