# Governed Resolution Plans & ActionGateway Execution

## Governed Execution
Approved resolution plans route material resolution actions (`resequence`, `change_window`, `change_scope`, `share_evidence`, `share_simulation`, `coordinate_review`, `change_dependency`, `replace_action`, `defer_action`, `accelerate_action`, `escalate_decision`) through `ActionGateway`.
Tracks partial resolution and residual conflicts.
