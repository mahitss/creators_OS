# Operational Incident Management & Anomaly Detection

## Anomaly Detection
- Compares 7-day/30-day historical baselines against real-time usage.
- Detects `cost_spike`, `latency_spike`, `failure_spike`, `token_spike`, and `retry_spike`.

## Incident Model
- Tracks service, severity, status (`detected`, `investigating`, `mitigated`, `resolved`), and source references without alert fatigue spam.
