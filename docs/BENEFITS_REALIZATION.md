# Enterprise Benefits Realization Engine

`Benefit` manages measurable benefits (`planned`, `measuring`, `on_track`, `at_risk`, `achieved`, `partially_achieved`, `not_achieved`, `unknown`) across 10 types (`financial`, `operational`, `customer`, `quality`, `risk_reduction`, `capacity`, `revenue`, `cost_reduction`, `time_saved`, `strategic`).

Integrates with `BenefitEvidence` to require verifiable data before declaring outcomes achieved.

## KPI Operating System 2.0 Integration
Integrates in Sprint 65 with Enterprise Performance Intelligence & KPI Operating System 2.0 (`KPI`, `KPIMeasurement`, `KPITarget`, `KPIVariance`). Links benefits directly to verifiable KPIs and measurement provenance.
