# Knowledge Assurance Planning & Portfolio-Level Risk Optimization 2.0

## Overview & Architecture

Vapor OS Sprint 96 moves from individual knowledge-risk remediation to portfolio-level assurance planning:

```
KNOWLEDGE RISKS → RISK AGGREGATION → PORTFOLIO EXPOSURE → ASSURANCE CAPACITY → REMEDIATION OPTIONS → SEQUENCING → SCENARIO ANALYSIS → ASSURANCE PLAN → HUMAN APPROVAL → EXECUTION → VERIFICATION → PORTFOLIO LEARNING
```

## Critical Principles
* **Assurance Planning Optimization Only**: Does not autonomously decide organizational priorities or budget allocations. Vapor aggregates, models, compares, simulates, prioritizes, sequences, recommends, tracks, and verifies. Humans retain explicit authority over funding, resource allocation, risk acceptance, governance, material remediation, and strategic prioritization.
* **Approved Terminology**: Describes capabilities as "portfolio-level assurance planning", "evidence-backed remediation prioritization", "capacity-aware knowledge risk planning", and "human-approved assurance plans".
