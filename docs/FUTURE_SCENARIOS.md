# Future Scenarios & Plausibility Framework

`FutureScenario` constructs plausible future scenarios across 6 types (`baseline`, `upside`, `downside`, `disruption`, `transformation`, `custom`) and 5 horizons (`1_year`, `3_year`, `5_year`, `10_year`, `custom`).

Plausibility (`low`, `medium`, `high`, `unknown`) is explicitly tracked separate from probability to emphasize scenario exploration over statistical forecasting.
