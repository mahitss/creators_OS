# Operational Scenes, Timelines & Causal Maps

## Operational Scene Definition
A coherent operational situation grouping signals, risks, plans, conflicts, interventions, decisions, and dependencies.

## Scene Status & Causal Relationships
Status transitions through `forming, active, escalating, stabilizing, resolved, archived`. Relationships tracked strictly as `causes, contributes_to, depends_on, blocks, mitigates, follows, correlates_with, unknown`. Does not infer causation without evidence.
