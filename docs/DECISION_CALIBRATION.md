# Decision Calibration & Quality Metrics

Details confidence calibration and quality scoring in Vapor OS.

## Quality Metrics
- **Evidence Quality Score**: Completeness and freshness of supporting sources.
- **Option Coverage Score**: Diversity of evaluated options.
- **Constraint Compliance Score**: Strict adherence to hard constraints.
- **Risk Coverage Score**: Thoroughness of multi-dimensional risk matrix.
- **Calibration Error**: Measures difference between predicted confidence and actual outcome success rate.
