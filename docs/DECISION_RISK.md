# Multi-Dimensional Decision Risk Model

Details risk dimension tracking for decisions in Vapor OS.

## Risk Dimensions
- **Financial Risk**: Cost impact and budget variance.
- **Security Risk**: Access boundary and DLP policy compliance.
- **Operational Risk**: Maintenance burden and system uptime impact.
- **Data Risk**: Confidentiality and governance boundary exposure.
- **Compliance Risk**: Regulatory and audit policy adherence.
- **Execution Risk**: Implementation complexity and dependency risk.
- **Reputational Risk**: External stakeholder impact.
