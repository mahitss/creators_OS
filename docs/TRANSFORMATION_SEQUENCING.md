# Transformation Sequencing & Trade-off Robustness

`TransformationSequence` and `TransformationSequenceComparison` evaluate sequence alternatives across 7 strategy types (`fast`, `risk_first`, `capability_first`, `cost_first`, `resilience_first`, `balanced`, `custom`).

Compares time, cost, risk, capacity, benefit, and optionality trade-offs, computing robustness scores under stress conditions.
