# Enterprise Transformation Resilience Signal Correlation

## Cross-Signal Correlation & Causation Safeguards

Vapor correlates signals across capacity, dependency, risk, recovery, benefit, and governance domains.

## Causation Safeguard Rule
Correlation does NOT imply causation. Relationships are explicitly tagged as:
1. `observed_correlation`: Empirical statistical co-occurrence.
2. `hypothesized_relationship`: Modeled potential connection.
3. `confirmed_relationship`: Governed, verified architectural link.
