# Multi-Dimensional Risk Management

Details risk dimension tracking in Vapor OS Mission Orchestration.

## Risk Matrix
- **Data Risk**: Access level to sensitive enterprise data.
- **Action Risk**: External side effects and write operations.
- **Financial Risk**: Cost projection and tool invocation expenditure.
- **Security Risk**: DLP classification and tenant isolation boundaries.
- **Execution Risk**: Dependency complexity and agent reliability rating.

Scores are exposed independently without collapsing into a single opaque rating.
