# Recommendation Quality & Decision Quality Dimensions

## Recommendation Quality Dimensions
Assesses separately:
- Evidence Quality
- Scenario Quality
- Risk Calibration
- Coverage Accuracy
- Timeline Accuracy
- Capacity Accuracy
- Uncertainty Calibration

## Decision Quality Dimensions
Evaluates decisions across information sufficiency, option completeness, trade-off visibility, uncertainty visibility, governance alignment, and outcome alignment. Does not reduce to a single score.
