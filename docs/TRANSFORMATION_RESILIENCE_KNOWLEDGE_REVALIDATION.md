# Human-Governed Revalidation & Knowledge Narrowing

## Revalidation Actions & Applicability Narrowing
* **Revalidation Results**: `confirmed, updated, narrowed, contested, invalidated, inconclusive`.
* **Applicability Narrowing**: When a lesson remains valid under specific conditions, the system narrows applicability instead of invalidating the entire lesson.
* **History Preservation**: Retirement and state changes preserve historical decision records without hard deletion.
