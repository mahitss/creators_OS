# Enterprise Transformation Resilience Structural Change

## Structural Change Classification

Detects major structural shifts across the transformation ecosystem:
* **Vendor Concentration**: Increased reliance on a single third-party provider across transformation waves.
* **Capacity Reduction**: Loss of engineering FTE or SME availability.
* **Dependency Topology Changes**: Addition or removal of core infrastructure dependencies.
* **Materiality Classification**: Minor, material, major, or critical.
