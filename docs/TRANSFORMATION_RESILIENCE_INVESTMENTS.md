# Enterprise Transformation Resilience Investments

## Portfolio-Level Resilience Investment Optimization

Cross-portfolio resilience investments are evaluated based on their scope of protection:
* **Single Transformation Protection**: Localized resilience improvements.
* **Multi-Transformation Protection**: Investments shielding multiple parallel waves.
* **Portfolio & Enterprise Protection**: Foundation-level investments protecting the entire enterprise.

## Trade-off Analysis
Compares cost, risk reduction percentage, capacity requirements, benefit preservation, and optionality value without collapsing into a single score.
