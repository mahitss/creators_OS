# Enterprise Transformation Resilience Decision Packets

## Review-Ready Executive Decision Packets

Generates complete decision packets for leadership review containing:
* **Supporting Evidence**: Telemetry and observation sources.
* **Scenario Results**: Output ranges from digital twin simulations.
* **Trade-Off Analysis**: Cost, risk reduction, and capacity requirements.
* **Uncertainty Bounds**: Explicitly stated confidence intervals.
* **Recommendations & Alternatives**: Viable options with required governance approval paths.
