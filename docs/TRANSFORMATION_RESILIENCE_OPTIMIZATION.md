## Overview & Architecture

Vapor OS Sprint 107 builds the multi-objective optimization and portfolio strategy layer above Sprints 104-106:

```
STRESS TEST RESULTS + DIGITAL TWIN SNAPSHOT + OBJECTIVES/CONSTRAINTS + PARETO FRONTIER + TRADE-OFF ANALYSIS + ANALYTICAL RECOMMENDATION
```

## Critical Principle
OPTIMIZATION IS DECISION SUPPORT. Vapor analyzes, ranks, compares, simulates, recommends, and optimizes under constraints. Vapor must NOT autonomously allocate budgets, reassign people, change strategic priorities, approve investments, or accept risk.
