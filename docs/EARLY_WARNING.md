# Early Warning & Audited Suppression

`EarlyWarning` dispatches early warnings with priority ratings (`low`, `medium`, `high`, `critical`) and status tracking (`new`, `acknowledged`, `investigating`, `mitigating`, `resolved`, `expired`, `false_positive`).

Temporary warning suppression requires an explicit reason, authorized actor, expiry, and full audit logging (`ThreatFalsePositive`).
