# Operator Roles & Role-Based Control Views

## Role-Based Access Control
- **Executive**: Read-only high-level business health, active risks, budget tracking, and decision summaries.
- **Operations Engineer**: Full visibility into Services, Incidents, Workflows, Events, Agents, and low/medium risk control actions.
- **Security Administrator**: Specialized view for DLP findings, policy denials, identity sessions, and security control actions.
- **Auditor**: Read-only compliance access to control action logs, change timelines, and decision records.
