# Multi-Dimensional Risk Assessment Model

Details risk classification in Vapor OS.

## 7 Risk Dimensions
- **Data Risk**: Data classification and boundary exposure.
- **Financial Risk**: Cost impact and financial transactions.
- **Security Risk**: Access escalation and destructive actions.
- **Privacy Risk**: PII and sensitive data exposure.
- **Operational Risk**: System uptime and infrastructure stability.
- **Compliance Risk**: Regulatory compliance and audit policy.
- **Reputational Risk**: External communications and public actions.
