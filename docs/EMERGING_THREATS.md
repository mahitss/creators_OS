# Emerging Threats & Probability Ranges

`EmergingThreat` tracks emerging threats across 7 statuses (`emerging`, `watch`, `escalating`, `contained`, `materialized`, `dismissed`, `expired`).

Uses probability ranges (e.g. `40-60%`) and time horizons (`hours`, `days`, `weeks`, `months`, `unknown`) supported by `ThreatEvidence` and `ThreatDriver`.
