# Enterprise Transformation Resilience Investment Engine

## Investment Candidate Evaluation

Resilience improvements are formulated into formal investment candidates containing:
* **Problem Statement**: Explicit description of underlying vulnerability or single point of failure.
* **Proposed Improvement**: Detailed engineering or governance enhancement.
* **Financial Investment**: Upfront and ongoing operational cost estimates.
* **Risk Reduction Percentage**: Quantified reduction in disruption probability or severity.
* **Uncertainty Level**: Empirical confidence rating (`low`, `medium`, `high`).
* **Multi-Transformation Protection**: Identification of all downstream transformations protected by a single resilience investment.

## Approval & Policy Controls
All investment candidates require explicit human executive authorization via `PolicyEngine` and `Approval` workflows.
