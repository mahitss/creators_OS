# Critical Capabilities & Business Impact Profiles

`CriticalCapability` identifies essential organizational capabilities with explicit owner, status, and criticality (`low`, `medium`, `high`, `critical`).

`BusinessImpactProfile` tracks financial, operational, customer, regulatory, reputational, and strategic impacts alongside Maximum Tolerable Disruption (MTD), Tolerable Downtime, Recovery Time Objective (RTO), and Data Recovery Objective (RPO). Values are empirical, never fabricated.
